# Sovereignty Gate Principle
## Ditch AI Individual Sovereignty Standard v0.1

> Gates check material compatibility, not identity. Default preserves the
> position of the person with least leverage. Override is always available
> to the rights-holder.

---

### Origin

A fuel can with NFC chemistry-matching raised the question: what happens
when a compatibility gate becomes an eligibility gate? The pump-hold
feature checks whether this fuel will damage this engine. It never checks
whether this person is allowed to buy fuel. Missing tag means no check
means fuel flows. Override is always available. If a licensee extends it
to gate fuel access on non-chemistry criteria, the license terminates.

The same primitive governs every gate in the Acequia Protocol. The
mayordomo measures the water, not the farmer.

---

### Three rules

**1. Material question only.** Every gate in the protocol checks material
compatibility, authorization scope, or measurement. Never identity,
worthiness, standing, or compliance status beyond the scope of the
specific transaction.

**2. Default protects least leverage.** The default state -- when a tag
is missing, a credential is absent, or a system is down -- preserves the
position of the person who can least afford the gate going wrong. For
fuel: flow. For data: no flow. The default is not always open or always
closed. It is always protective of the individual.

**3. Override to rights-holder.** Override is always available to the
person whose rights are at stake. Not to the system operator. Not to the
vendor. Not to the platform. The parciante can open or close their own
compuerta. A system that removes override from the rights-holder is
non-conforming regardless of intent.

### Application by flow

#### Supply chain

The attestation checks "does this component meet the spec." Not "is this
vendor in the network." A non-attested component flows -- the buyer
assumes risk, the receipt says UNATTESTED, and the gate does not block.

**Non-conforming:** An attestation system that becomes a vendor approval
club. Network membership as a condition of sale. Attestation fees that
price out small suppliers. Any structure where the gate's real function
is market access rather than material verification.

#### Community data

The headgate checks "did the community authorize this data flow at this
scope." Not "is this requester a member of our vendor network." Default
is no flow -- because unauthorized data flow is what harms the
individual.

**Non-conforming:** A headgate that opens on vendor partnership rather
than community authorization. A system where the community cannot close
the gate. Access tiers based on payment rather than authorization scope.
Any structure where data flows because of who is asking rather than what
was authorized.

#### Water

Measurement checks volume and timing. The repartimiento shares shortage
by proportion of rights, not by standing or ability to pay. The gate
reads the flow rate, not the farmer's name.

**Non-conforming:** Allocation by anything other than rights and
proportion. Shortage that falls on whoever has least leverage. A
measurement system that reports different numbers depending on who is
reading. Any structure where the answer to "how much water" changes based
on "whose water."

#### Labor

Seat eligibility checks contribution. The ditch was built by the people
who used it. If you did the work, you hold a seat. The gate checks "did
you build, maintain, or operate infrastructure in this mesh." Not "do you
hold a credential from an institution we recognize."

**Non-conforming:** A table that requires institutional credentials
instead of demonstrated contribution. Seat access gated by geography,
nationality, employer, or network membership. Any structure where "you
did the work" is not sufficient to hold a seat.

#### AI governance

The score measures whether the balance between human and machine control
is intentional. A high-autonomy system with documented, chosen
architecture is conforming. A low-autonomy system with no documentation
is non-conforming. The score does not judge the balance -- it measures
whether someone chose it.

**Non-conforming:** A score that enforces a preferred balance. A
conformance test that fails systems for having "too much" or "too little"
automation. Any structure where the governance instrument becomes a
preference enforcer rather than an intentionality measure.

---

### Anti-telos (what this prevents)

The gate starts checking identity. The attestation becomes a vendor club.
The headgate becomes a network membership check. The measurement becomes
an allocation lever. The seat becomes a credential gate. The score
becomes a preference enforcer.

In every case the mechanism is the same: a material gate acquires a
social function it was not designed for, and the person with least
leverage pays first. The fuel can that checks chemistry starts checking
compliance. The headgate that checks authorization starts checking
membership. The attestation that checks quality starts checking
affiliation.

The principle is: if the gate can answer "who are you" as well as "what
is this," someone will eventually use the first answer instead of the
second. Sovereignty gate means the gate cannot ask the question.

---

### Conformance

A Ditch AI specification, contract, or certified implementation is
**sovereignty-gate conforming** when:

1. Every gate in the system can be described entirely in terms of
   material properties, authorization scope, or measurement -- with no
   reference to the identity of the party beyond what is necessary to
   verify authorization.
2. The default state of every gate protects the person with least
   leverage in that transaction.
3. Override is available to the rights-holder without requiring
   vendor/operator/platform cooperation.
4. No gate collects or retains identity information beyond the scope of
   the current transaction.

A licensee that extends any gate to check identity, standing, or
compliance status beyond material scope is non-conforming. License
implications per the participation agreement.

### Relation to existing mechanisms

| Mechanism | Sovereignty gate constraint |
| --- | --- |
| Attestation firewall | Checks product against spec. Never checks vendor against roster. |
| Neutral Contract | Contract survives vendor change. Override: principal can exit with records. |
| Provenance | Chain is receipts. Never gatekeeping. Non-attested goods flow with UNATTESTED flag. |
| Shortage (repartimiento) | Shared by proportion. Never by leverage, standing, or ability to pay. |
| Headgate | Authorization scope only. Never network membership. Default: closed (protects individual). |
| Governed Autonomy score | Measures intentionality. Never enforces preference. |
