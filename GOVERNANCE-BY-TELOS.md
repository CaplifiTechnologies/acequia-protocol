# Ditch AI Governs by Telos + Corpus

Ditch AI is an open standards body. Its governance architecture is telos-bound.

## The connection

The Acequia Protocol's headgate is a telos-bound gate. It does not ask "what does the operator want?" It asks "what is this infrastructure for?" The answer — community sovereignty over community-generated data — is the telos. Every gate decision flows from it.

This is not a metaphor. The headgate's behavior is identical to the telos governance kernel specified in HP-001 and SPEC-v0.1:

- **Deterministic gate** — nothing flows without authorization. Same architecture as `telos.py gate`.
- **Append-only audit** — every access logged, integrity verifiable. Same as the telos amendment trail.
- **Community authorization** — the parciantes (community) set policy. Same as the human-only rule: teloi must be submitted by humans, never agents.
- **Polarity declaration** — fail-closed by default; opening requires explicit community vote. Same as HP-001 primitive #3.
- **Governed federation** — inter-jurisdictional queries require mutual authorization. Same as telos constellation precedence: conflicts between jurisdictions resolve through declared rules, not vendor terms.

## What Ditch AI publishes

Ditch AI publishes specifications. Those specifications are telos-bound: every spec declares the telos it serves, and compliance is measured against that purpose, not against the letter of the rules.

This makes Ditch AI a **telos commons publisher** — the entity described in Phase 6 of the ALMI telos build plan. Published teloi that any community (or agent) can bind to:

- "Community-generated data is civic infrastructure" — a telos any municipality can adopt
- "Custody belongs to the institution that cannot be acquired" — a telos libraries can bind to
- "Access requires measurement" — a telos operators are certified against

These are not rules. They are purposes. A compliant operator is one whose infrastructure serves the declared purpose, not one who checked every box on a list.

## The Corpus

Ditch AI's governance corpus is the body of published specifications, attestations, and governance decisions. It is the Corpus of Telos for community data infrastructure — the same relationship that ALMI's Corpus of Intent (now migrating to Corpus of Telos) has to the ALMI agent stack.

The corpus is append-only. Amendments to specifications are additive, never destructive. The trail is the truth.

## Relationship to ALMI

Ditch AI is not a module of ALMI. ALMI is Matt's personal agent infrastructure. Ditch AI is a public standards body. They share governance architecture (telos-bound gates, append-only audit, human-only authority) because they were designed by the same person from the same principles.

ALMI may operate as a certified mayordomo under the Acequia Protocol. Ditch AI sets the standard; ALMI (or anyone) implements it.
