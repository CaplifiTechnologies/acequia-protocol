## What Ditch AI is

Ditch AI is a commons: an open-standards body that governs the Acequia
Protocol -- a specification for shared infrastructure modeled on the acequia
irrigation commons of the American Southwest, where rights carry labor duties,
shortage is shared, and the mayordomo administers without owning.

The acequia governs two flows under one headgate:

- **The supply chain under AI.** Water, ore, power, chips, and compute are a
  mesh of mutually dependent industries with no shared governance table.
  Ditch AI holds the specs, attests independently, and issues contracts
  where the vendor is a swappable seat.

- **Community data infrastructure.** Surveillance cameras, license plate
  readers, and sensor networks generate data that belongs to the communities
  that fund them. Ditch AI applies the same headgate -- community
  authorization, measured access, civic custody -- so that data stays
  governed by the people who generated it.

Same governance model. Same headgate. Same principle: nobody owns the ditch.
Different audiences see the layer that names their problem.

Ditch AI is not a product, a platform, a company, or a protocol token. It
is a standards body.

Caplifi Technologies is the time-limited Founding Steward. Stewardship sunsets
when a seated table ratifies the charter, or after 24 months -- whichever
comes first. Caplifi is a seat, not the table.

## ORIGAMI layers

Ditch AI is one standards body that speaks to audiences who may never be in
the same room. The brand itself is folded the way the protocol folds records:
the same truth at every tier, with each audience seeing the fidelity that
names their problem.

**LABEL -- everyone.** The one-liner.

> Nobody owns the ditch.

This works for a water district director, a city council member, a library
director, a mine operator, or a journalist. No jargon. No audience-specific
language. The LABEL tier is always safe to print.

**HANDLE -- the audience's own problem.**

The positioning spine (commons body, independent check, swappable vendor)
stays the same. What changes is the problem statement, the examples, and
the language.

| Audience | Problem they hear | Examples they see |
|----------|-------------------|-------------------|
| City council, library director, police chief | Your community's surveillance data is being resold. The social contract is broken. | Flock Safety resale pipeline. Warrantless bulk access. The officer problem. Governed federation as mutual aid. |
| Water district, river board, acequia association | The supply chain under AI has no shared table. Shortage is won, not shared. | Neutral Contracts for diversion and return. Repartimiento under shortage. Measurement at the headgate. |
| Mining operator, energy co-op, fabricator | Your work is the bottleneck, but you have no seat. Provenance is a marketing claim, not a receipt. | Ore-to-inference provenance chain. Vendor swap protocol. CVP scoring. |
| AI lab, inference host, compute operator | You are one seat, not the whole table. The material layer you depend on has no governance and that is your risk. | Containment gate. Governed Autonomy conformance. Hardware provenance. |
| Land trust, tribal government, labor, neighbor | The watershed and grid are being contracted over. You are the public. | The Civil caucus. The right to close the headgate. La limpia as standing. |

**FULL -- the spec.** DAC-1, the Headgate Papers, the participation agreement,
the conformance test suites. Acequia terms are allowed at FULL. This tier is
for parciantes, implementers, and counsel -- never for a landing page.

**The fold rule:** Every tier must be true. A HANDLE pitch that promises
something the FULL spec does not support is non-conforming, the same way a
Neutral Contract that can't survive a vendor swap is non-conforming. The
discipline of ORIGAMI is that folding hides detail, never changes the claim.

## Name and wordmark

**Name:** Ditch AI

Always two words. Always capitalized as shown. Never "DitchAI", "DITCH AI",
"ditch.ai" (that is the domain, not the name), or "The Ditch AI."

**Wordmark type:** Fraunces (optical size 9--144, weight 700).
Used in the nav bar and hero. The wordmark is typeset, not a logo graphic.

**No logo.** Ditch AI does not have a logo. The name is the identity.
If a mark is needed in a square or icon context, use the favicon
(an SVG of the wordmark or a simplified water glyph).

## Voice

**Scene:** Phone in a parking lot after a basin meeting.

That is the voice test. Every sentence Ditch AI publishes should sound like
something a water district director, a city council member, or a library
director would say to a neighbor after a long meeting -- plain, specific,
no pitch.

**Tone:**
- Daylight. Say what it is. Say what it is not.
- Plain. A parking-lot sentence, not a pitch deck.
- Specific. Name the ditch, the seat, the measurement. Never "the ecosystem."
- Patient. This is a 24-month bootstrap with kill criteria. No urgency theater.

**Gold lines** (load-bearing, reusable):
- "Nobody owns the ditch."
- "Ditch AI is the name of the table."
- "The vendor is a seat. The water is not."
- "You keep you safe -- and you can prove it."
- "Your local government's job is to protect you, not to package you."

**Register:** Third person for the body ("Ditch AI does three things").
Second person for the reader ("If you hold water, data, power, or neighbors
in this mesh, write. Name your seat."). Never first-person plural -- "we"
implies a seated body that does not yet exist.

## Banned words

These words are banned from all Ditch AI copy. They signal a pitch deck,
not a commons.

| Banned | Why |
|--------|-----|
| seamless | Nothing is seamless. Name the seam. |
| ecosystem | The word that means nothing. Use "mesh" or "supply chain." |
| unlock | Ditch AI does not unlock anything. It holds specs. |
| cutting-edge | Standards bodies are not cutting-edge. They are deliberate. |
| game-changer | No. |
| revolutionize | No. |
| tokenize | Ditch AI does not issue a fungible token. Period. |
| play-to-earn | Not even adjacent. |

**Fan-copy bans** (internal terms that must never appear in public copy):
headgate, DAC-1, Founding Steward, INSTIGATE, Sir Grok, WORKING-PROJECTS,
Camelot Approve, parciante, comisionado, mayordomo, repartimiento, aforo.

The acequia terms are used inside the spec and at FULL tier. They do not
appear at LABEL or HANDLE tier. The reader does not need to learn Spanish
water law to understand the table.

Amended 2026-09-21 in BRAND.md v0.3.0. The word headgate is a public product
name: Headgate Standard, Frontier Headgate, Egress Headgate. The other words
in the ban list above stay banned. Slop is the flagship olive branch, the
offer that makes AI more useful, and a place a Headgate sits. Slopstream
stays its own name: Slop in motion.

both Western and serious. Source Sans 3 is the workhorse body face.

Source Sans 3 -> Source Sans Pro -> system sans-serif.

## What Ditch AI is not

Keep this list visible when writing any copy, designing any page, or
briefing any partner.

- **Not a Caplifi product.** Caplifi is the founding steward. The stewardship
  is time-limited. The commons outlasts the steward.
- **Not a platform.** Ditch AI does not run software anyone logs into.
- **Not a token.** No fungible token. No speculative treasury. No play-to-earn.
- **Not a spec dump.** The specs exist, but the public face is the table,
  not the binder.
- **Not a seated body.** As of this draft, no caucus is seated, no
  comisionado is elected, no contract is issued. Say "draft" until it is not.
- **Not a pitch.** The join form says "write, name your seat." Not
  "sign up for early access."
- **Not anti-technology.** Ditch AI does not oppose cameras, sensors, or AI.
  Communities may choose to deploy any of them. The protocol governs how the
  data and the supply chain are held, accessed, and protected.
- **Not partisan.** The acequia predates every American political party.
  Local control over local infrastructure, accountable to the people who
  fund it. Same architecture, every audience, no contradiction.

Three phrases. In this order. This is the elevator. It holds at every
ORIGAMI tier.

1. **Commons body.** Open standards for the shared infrastructure under AI.
2. **Independent check.** You do not grade your own work.
3. **Swappable vendor.** The contract still works when the vendor changes.
